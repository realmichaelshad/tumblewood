    How it is made

    The ramps are solid European beech. Each groove is routed in one pass and sanded until a marble runs down it without a rattle.

    Each chime is an aluminum bar on soft mounts. A bar's length sets its note, so the eight are cut to a five-note scale: any run you build stays in tune.

    The bells are cast brass. The board is birch plywood with a peg hole every inch, finished with oil and beeswax.
